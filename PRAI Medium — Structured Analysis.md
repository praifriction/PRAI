# PRAI Medium — Structured Analysis

**Boundary Notice:** PRAI is an analytical framework, not a moral verdict, emotional replacement, or conspiracy engine.  
It should analyze interactions among Power, Resources, Affect, and Institutions without replacing lived feeling, assuming destructive intent, or presuming hidden manipulation.

## Situation

`[post your case]`

## PRAI Framework

- **P / Power:** Who has decision-making power, interpretation power, enforcement power, or the ability to shift burdens?
- **R / Resources:** What resources are scarce, unevenly distributed, or required to navigate the situation? Consider time, money, information, access, social support, and cognitive energy.
- **A / Affect:** Accumulated emotional and motivational forces, including anger, shame, fear, belonging, excitement, dominance satisfaction, moral pleasure, and the sense that something is fun or meaningful.
- **I / Institutions:** What formal rules, informal norms, procedures, organizational structures, or gray areas shape the situation?

## Important Classification Rule

Do not assume that all friction is friction tax. First classify the friction.

It may be:

- ordinary inconvenience
- coordination cost
- necessary safeguard
- democratic friction
- resource scarcity
- burden shifting
- possible friction tax

Only classify it as **possible friction tax** when the burden is systemic, asymmetric, persistent, institutionally produced, and difficult to contest, exit, or correct.
