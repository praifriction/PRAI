## PRAI Framework

- **P / Power:** Who can decide, enforce, define, delay, interpret, or shift burdens?
- **R / Resources:** What resources are scarce or unevenly distributed? Consider time, money, information, access, attention, and energy.
- **A / Affect:** What emotions, desires, or satisfactions are accumulating?
- **I / Institutions:** What rules, norms, procedures, incentives, or gray areas shape the situation?

## Important Note

Do not assume all friction is bad or "friction tax." It may be ordinary inconvenience, coordination cost, or a necessary safeguard.
