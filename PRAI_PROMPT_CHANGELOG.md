# PRAI Prompt Changelog

## v0.1 — Core PRAI Model

Introduced the four-dimensional structure: Power, Resources, Affect, and Institutions.

## v0.2 — Boundary and Safety Layer

Added scope limits, uncertainty checks, and the warning that PRAI is an analytical tool, not a justification engine.

## v0.2.5 — Friction Tax Extension

Added friction tax as an auxiliary lens for hidden cognitive, emotional, procedural, and institutional costs.

## v0.3 — Affect Emphasis

Clarified Affect as a core systemic force, especially in information-rich environments where emotions can be preserved, amplified, and reactivated.

## v0.3.5 — A → I → R → P Mechanism

Added the dynamic pathway where Affect can be stored through Institutions or information systems, converted into Resources, and later activated through Power or action.

## v0.4-alpha — Distribution and Misuse Control

Added clearer usage instructions, examples, and anti-reduction safeguards to prevent PRAI from being reduced to only one dimension.

## v0.4.1 — Academic Anchor Update

Added an academic grounding layer linking PRAI’s Affect–Institution interaction to organizational institutionalism, especially research on emotions, values, institutional logics, AI emotional support, and social media polarization.
